# MORPHED TECH — Brand Guidelines

## Logo Concept

The **M** lettermark features an abstract **Trishul-inspired** center stroke:
- The outer M shape represents **structure and engineering**
- The center trishul prongs represent **discipline, clarity, and transformation**
- The diamond tip represents **precision and mastery**
- The design is geometric, modern, and technology-focused — no religious styling

## Usage Rules

1. **Minimum size**: Icon 16px, Full logo 120px wide
2. **Clear space**: Maintain padding equal to the M height around logo
3. **Never**: Stretch, rotate, add effects, change colors
4. **Backgrounds**: Use on solid dark (#0F172A) or solid white only
5. **Favicon**: Use simplified version for 16x16 and 32x32

---

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter (800 weight for "MORPHED", 300 for "TECH")
- **Fallback**: system-ui, sans-serif
- **Letter spacing**: 1.5-2px for brand name
